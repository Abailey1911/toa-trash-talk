# ToA Trash Talk

A RuneLite plugin that makes the Tombs of Amascut a little sillier.

- **Kephri throws it back.** When Kephri's dung attack knocks you back, the chat line
  "Kephri throws you back!" becomes "Kephri throws it back!" (you can change the text).
- **Every boss trash-talks you.** Akkha, Ba-Ba, Kephri, Zebak and both Wardens say
  something over their heads when you meet them, every so often during the fight, when
  they drop to a quarter health and when they die. Kephri also gloats when she dungs you.

Everything is client-side: only you see it. Each part can be turned off in the plugin's
settings, along with how often the bosses talk and whether their lines also go in chat.
